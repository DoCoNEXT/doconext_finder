# Code of Conduct

This repository is DoCoNEXT's public issue tracker for Finder. It does not take
code contributions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), so what it hosts
is bug reports, feature requests, and the discussion around them. This page
says what we expect there, and what happens when it is not met.

## What we expect

- **Be precise, and be kind.** A report that says what you did, what happened
  and what you expected is the most useful thing you can give us, and it costs
  nothing to write it politely.
- **Argue about the software, not the person.** Disagreement about a design is
  welcome. Contempt is not.
- **Respect people's time.** Do not reopen a closed issue to restate it, do not
  pull maintainers into unrelated threads, and do not demand a timeline — we
  would rather tell you honestly how long something will take than promise a
  date (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- **Keep other people's data out of it.** Finder searches people's files, and
  this tracker is public. Strip names, file names, identifiers and document
  contents from logs and screenshots before posting them.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language, publishing
someone's private information, threats, and sustained disruption of a
discussion. This applies to everyone here, the maintainers included.

## Where this applies

Everything under this repository: issues and their comments, and security
reports sent through the Security tab or to the address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or delete a comment, lock a thread, close an issue, or
block an account from the repository, without prior warning where the
behaviour is clear. We will say why.

To report someone's behaviour, or to contest a decision taken under this page,
write to **support@doconext.com**. A report is read by a person, kept
confidential, and answered.
